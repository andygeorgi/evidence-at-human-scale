# Public records (EN01–EN05)

Public records are cited directly. Each is a **dated single case**, not evidence of a systemic failure, and none is a statement about any authority's staff, systems or current practice. Original titles are in Icelandic; English glosses are the author's and are not official translations. Nothing here was provided by, reviewed by or endorsed by the authority discussed.

All five were fetched and the cited passage checked in the original language on 23 November 2026.

<a id="en01"></a>
## EN01 · Monitoring capacity

- **Source:** National Audit Office of Iceland (Ríkisendurskoðun), *Eftirlit Fiskistofu: eftirfylgni* (follow-up audit of fisheries monitoring), follow-up audit report to Parliament, May 2023.
- **URL:** <https://www.rikisend.is/reskjol/files/Skyrslur/2023-Fiskistofa-eftirfylgni.pdf>
- **Passage used:** the finding that, with more emphasis on electronic monitoring of real-time data, the authority needs enough staff to process that information and respond to deviations.
- **Limits:** dated finding; current status unknown.
- **Used for:** the attention problem ([chapter 2, observation 1](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md)).

<a id="en02"></a>
## EN02 · Correction propagation

- **Source:** Ministry of Industries (Iceland), appeal ruling of 14 April 2026 on a special fee for over-catch in coastal fishing.
- **URL:** <https://www.stjornarradid.is/gogn/urskurdir-og-alit-/stakur-urskurdur/2026-04-14-Urskurdur-um-akvordun-Fiskistofu-um-alagningu-serstaks-gjalds-vegna-umframveidi-a-strandveidum/>
- **Passage used:** the landing-date correction of March 2024, the authority's statement that the fee decision could have been reopened after it, and the ruling that the fee overstated the over-catch once the landing was correctly dated, which annulled the decision of 24 August 2023 and remitted the case.
- **Limits:** single documented case; later outcome unknown.
- **Used for:** [C02](../contracts/c02-evidence.md) lifecycle, invalidation of derivatives and pending approvals, and EV12 ([chapter 2, observation 3](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md); [chapter 14](../docs/paper/14-start-monday.md)).

<a id="en03"></a>
## EN03 · Rule basis

- **Source:** Ministry of Industries (Iceland), appeal ruling of 7 November 2025 on the cancellation of catch shares.
- **URL:** <https://www.stjornarradid.is/efst-a-baugi/frettir/stok-frett/2025-11-07-Akvordun-Fiskistofu-um-nidurfellingu-aflahlutdeilda/>
- **Passage used:** the notice that began when the catch-registration system indicated under-fishing, and the finding that the decision rested on the wrong legal basis because the reference period is the fishing year.
- **Limits:** single documented case. The paper does not claim that the system's own rule was wrong; the engagement records mark that as inference.
- **Used for:** deterministic findings that declare rule version, inputs, reference period and scope ([chapter 2, observation 4](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md)).

<a id="en04"></a>
## EN04 · Lawful purpose

- **Source:** Data Protection Authority of Iceland (Persónuvernd), ruling of 28 March 2023 in case 2021030579.
- **URL:** <https://island.is/s/personuvernd/urskurdir-akvardanir-og-alit/eftirlit-af-halfu-fiskistofu>
- **Passage used:** drone video monitoring of a fishing vessel without a legal basis under the law then in force, and the duties of transparency and information.
- **Limits:** single documented case.
- **Used for:** purpose and permission as enforceable inputs ([chapter 2, observation 5](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md)).

<a id="en05"></a>
## EN05 · Non-AI alternatives

- **Source:** Data Protection Authority of Iceland (Persónuvernd), *Þróun og innleiðing* (developing and introducing AI).
- **URL:** <https://island.is/s/personuvernd/throun-og-innleiding>
- **Passage used:** *Tilgangur og þörf* (purpose and need), which asks whether other solutions without AI can achieve the same aim and notes that such alternatives may be less risky or more suitable.
- **Limits:** undated page, first retrieved 16 October 2026 and re-checked 23 November 2026.
- **Used for:** non-AI and zero-specialist baselines ([chapter 2, observation 5](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md); [chapter 7](../docs/paper/07-one-interaction.md)).

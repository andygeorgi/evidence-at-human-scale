# Engagement records (IN01–IN04)

The architecture was informed by a case-based engagement. Four of the engagement's own working records support observations 2, 6 and 7 and the coverage lesson in [chapter 2](../docs/paper/02-engagement.md). They are **described here, not published**: the records themselves are the author's private working papers.

## What is described, and how

- **IN01.** Agent-value test of the agents proposed by the case brief, 16 October 2026. No agent reached evidenced value on its own; evidence-backed value also came from non-agent controls and platform continuity; and the EN02 correction gap was logged as a design requirement.
- **IN02.** Re-planning brief, 10 November 2026, moving from agents producing additional outputs to one assistant that makes existing information consumable.
- **IN03.** Root-cause report on the demonstrator's first cloud deployment, 6 November 2026.
- **IN04.** Demonstrator acceptance record, 17 November 2026, on synthetic data.

IN01, IN03 and IN04 were AI-assisted and, where reviewed, AI-reviewed; IN02 is the author's own brief. The case brief that opened the engagement, including the agents it proposed, is unpublished engagement material and is treated as unverified. None of these records measures the benefit of this architecture.

## Evidence class and limits

| ID | Evidence class | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IN01 | Author's AI-assisted assessment | One internal test within a single case-based engagement; not an independent evaluation and not statistical evidence. |
| IN02 | The author's own decision | A decision record, not evidence of effect. |
| IN03 | Real internal record; demonstrator infrastructure, synthetic data | Describes one deployment tool and one failure. |
| IN04 | Real internal record; synthetic data | Technical acceptance only. |

## Why they are not published

They are the author's private working papers. The paper relies on the generalized lessons and their stated limits, not on the records themselves. Nothing in this repository should be read as a claim that the authority, a customer or any third party took part in, approved or validated these records.

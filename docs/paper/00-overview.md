# Overview: systems produce evidence, AI makes it consumable, people remain accountable

[Paper contents](README.md) · [Next: the scarce resource is human attention →](01-human-attention.md)

> **Systems produce evidence. AI makes it consumable. People remain accountable.**

Agentic systems can increase information production faster than people can increase their attention. More agents can create more interpretations; more summaries can merely move the bottleneck. The Decision Owner, the person accountable for the decision, still needs to understand what matters, what supports it, what is uncertain, and what they are entitled to decide. A fluent answer that erases those distinctions is not a decision context.

This architecture makes the governed decision context its reusable unit. Authorized, attributable outputs are selected for a defined task, synthesized within limits, and released as a versioned decision packet. One review interaction makes that context consumable without separating it from lineage, uncertainty, access restrictions, or accountable human authority. Mandatory obligations and consequential execution retain independent paths.

The design comes from a case-based architecture engagement on a public-sector fisheries-control authority in Iceland. Public audit findings and appeal rulings in that domain showed how decisions built on records and automated flags can go wrong; the engagement's own prototype tests on synthetic data showed similar risks for model output. [What the engagement showed](02-engagement.md) maps each observation to the design decision it informed, and [Start with one decision, not a platform](14-start-monday.md) gives the first practical steps.

This paper is an architecture proposition, not a deployment or benefit claim. Runtime and human evaluation have not been run. The [architecture reference](../../architecture/README.md) and the [contract reference](../../contracts/README.md) carry the exhaustive controls; this paper carries the argument and their essential architectural meaning.
